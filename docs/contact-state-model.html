How Linyup sees a contact

Four independent things are tracked about every person: how far they got on their journey with you, whether they belong to the club, what they pay for, and whether they're on your active roster. Nothing you do on one changes the others.

    How people arrive

    Four ways in start the journey. Four don't — a shop purchase, a form, a waitlist or a coach adding someone by hand — and those people join the journey later, when they book a trial or sign up.

    How the journey moves

    It only goes forward on its own. Stepping someone back is treated as fixing a mistake.

    MoveWhat causes it
    → Trial bookedthey book their first trial
    Trial booked → Trial attendedyou check them in
    → Joinedthey complete the signup form, or you mark them Joined — counted as a conversion
    Buying somethingchanges their plan, never their journey
    Archiving, or marking Externalchanges their lifecycle, never their journey — what they did stays done

    The lifecycle

    Only your roster gets your attention: reminders, invitations, automations, the contact count. Leads — a trial booked but not attended, a shop sign-up not yet paid — are on it, but don't count toward your contact limit until they come or pay. External keeps someone's history and lets them keep training with you, without any of that attention.
