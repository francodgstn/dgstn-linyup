A new studio gets a checklist called **Get set up** on the dashboard. It walks you through what you sell and how people book, in the order that makes each step easy. Every step links straight to the screen where you do it.

Until you are done, the dashboard shows **Finish setting up**, with the number of steps left. When the checklist is complete it says **Your studio is ready**.

:::tip[Not every step fits every studio]
Some steps have a way to close them without doing them, such as **We only take cash** or **No prices for now**. Closing a step counts as done, and **Undo** brings it back.
:::

## What you sell

1. **Add your activities.** An activity is what you offer: a class like _Beginners' Judo_, or an appointment like _Personal training_. See [Schedule & classes](/studio/schedule/).
2. **Set your prices.** Create the plans people buy, such as memberships or class packs. See [How pricing fits together](/studio/pricing/). If you don't sell anything online yet, choose **No prices for now**.
3. **Set up payments.** Connect Stripe so people can pay online, straight into your account. If you only take cash, choose **We only take cash**.

## Open the doors

4. **Put a bookable class on the calendar.** Schedule your first class with **Allow online booking** switched on, so it appears on your booking page.
5. **Fill in your bio link page.** This is your public front door: one link to share on Instagram, WhatsApp or a flyer.
6. **Check what your booking form asks.** Decide what you need to know from someone who books, such as a phone number or a date of birth.
7. **Check what people actually pay.** One screen shows every price a visitor or member will see, so you can check nothing is off before you share your link.

## Extra steps

These are optional and don't count towards your progress.

- **Add your logo and colours** to your public pages.
- **See all your public pages** and **Get your QR codes** to print or share.
- **Explore plugins** such as online courses or a kiosk. See [Plugins](/plugins/).
- **Set up an automation**, for example a follow-up after a first trial.
- **Check your payments page.**
- **Invite your coaches.** Available on the Studio plan and above. See [Team & roles](/studio/team/).
- **Star a favourite** in How-to, to keep a guide you use often close at hand.

:::note[Closed the checklist?]
You can bring it back any time: open **How-to** and use **Show it on the dashboard again** on the Setup checklist card.
:::
