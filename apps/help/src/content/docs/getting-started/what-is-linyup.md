Linyup runs the day-to-day of a studio, a club or an independent coach. It covers your schedule, the people you look after, what you sell and how you get paid, and the pages where new people find you and book. It works for any activity: martial arts, dance, yoga, swimming, fitness, or one-to-one coaching.

## Who uses which part

| Who | Where | What they do there |
|---|---|---|
| **Owners and managers** | The web app at [app.linyup.com](https://app.linyup.com) | Set up the studio, manage contacts, prices, payments and the team. |
| **Coaches** | The same web app, with the access you give them | See their day, run check-in, publish their availability and look after their people. |
| **Members and visitors** | Your [public pages](/public/) | Book a class, buy a plan, sign in to their member space. No Linyup account needed. |

Your members never create a password. When they need to sign in, for example to see their bookings, they get a code by email.

## What you can do with it

- **Schedule**: classes on a calendar, recurring series, appointments booked from a coach's availability, and events such as camps and gradings.
- **Contacts**: everyone your studio knows in one list, from first trial to long-time member, with who needs your attention today.
- **Selling**: memberships, class packs, drop-ins, trials and appointments, paid online through your own Stripe account.
- **Public pages**: a bio-link page, a booking page, a shop and a member space. Add a full website, or embed booking in the website you already have.
- **Plugins**: extras you switch on when you need them, such as online courses, a kiosk for your entrance, or finance reports for your accountant. See [Plugins](/plugins/).
