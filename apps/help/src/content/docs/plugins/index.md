Plugins are the parts of Linyup that not every studio needs. Your studio starts lean, and you switch on a plugin when you need what it adds. Switching one off hides it again; nothing else about your studio changes.

## Switch a plugin on

Go to **Settings → Plugins** and choose **Install** on the plugin you want. Only the studio's **owner** can install or remove plugins.

Which plugins your plan includes, and which you can add to your plan, is shown next to each one on that screen. Plugins marked **Beta** are new; they work, and we are still shaping them with the studios that use them.

## What's available

### Your public presence

| Plugin | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Website** | A public website for your studio, built from blocks: hero, about, gallery, live schedule, pricing and contact. |
| **Products** | Sell merchandise and equipment, with sizes and options, in your public shop. |
| **Gift Cards** | Sell gift cards in your shop or issue them at the desk. They pay for anything you charge for. |
| **Promo Codes** | Discount campaigns with codes people enter at checkout, with usage limits and validity dates. |
| **Custom Forms** | Build forms and collect answers publicly or from your contacts. |

### Members and engagement

| Plugin | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Online Courses** | Courses with text, audio and video lessons for your members. |
| **Gamification** | Points, streaks, badges and leaderboards. |
| **Referral Program** | Members earn rewards for bringing friends, tracked from the first booking to the reward. |
| **Contact Groups** | Nested groups such as sections, squads or committees. |
| **Custom Fields** | Your own fields on every contact: text, numbers, dates, dropdowns and checkboxes. |

### Running the front desk

| Plugin | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Kiosk** | A tablet at your entrance becomes a front-desk screen with the schedule, check-in QR and walk-in sign-up. See [Check-in & attendance](/coaches/check-in/#the-kiosk). |
| **WhatsApp** | Booking reminders and messages over WhatsApp, from your studio's own number. |

### Money and admin

| Plugin | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Finance & Accounting** | Monthly financial reports with a CSV export for your accountant, and a simple ledger. |
| **Asset register** | Keep track of what your club owns: equipment, fittings, vehicles and IT. |
| **QR-bill invoices** | Numbered invoices with a Swiss QR-bill, for members who pay by bank transfer. |
| **Health insurance receipts (Tarif 595)** | Reimbursement receipts your members can send to their Swiss health insurer. |

### AI and integrations

| Plugin | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **AI insights** | AI features you switch on one by one: a briefing on each contact, a recap you can email a member, and a reading of the whole team. |
| **AI assistant** | An in-app helper that answers how-to questions and points you to the right screen. |
| **API & AI connectors** | Connect Claude, ChatGPT and your own tools to your studio's data, read-only. See [Developers](/developers/). |
