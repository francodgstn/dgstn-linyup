Public pages are what people see without logging in, and they're where your new contacts and bookings actually come from. Each one is optional and switched on when you need it, so you only show what your studio uses.

| Page | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Bio-link** | Your link-in-bio landing page: one short URL that gathers your booking link, socials and key actions. The simplest thing to share on Instagram or in a bio. |
| **Website** | A fuller studio site you build from blocks (hero, schedule, pricing, gallery and more) at your public address. Turn it on when a single bio-link isn't enough. |
| **Booking page** | The public schedule anyone can book from. Visitors pick a class and a time and enter their details, and a contact plus a booking appear automatically. |
| **Shop** | A storefront for what you sell (subscriptions, products and courses), so people can buy without a back-and-forth. Appears once you have something to sell. |
| **Member space** | The logged-in area for your members: their subscription, bookings, profile and the courses they can open. |
| **Appointments** | Clients pick a coach, choose what they're booking and take a free time from that coach's published availability. Separate from the class booking page. |
| **Kiosk** | A simple full-screen view for an entrance tablet: today's schedule, a check-in QR code and walk-in sign-up. |

:::tip[Only what you need]
Public pages are plugins. Switch on a bio-link and a booking page today; when your first merch arrives, enabling the shop simply adds it. Nothing about the existing pages changes.
:::

## Booking from your own website

Already have a website? You can open the Linyup booking flow as a pop-up on your own pages. See [Embed booking on your website](/developers/embed-booking/).
