Your schedule is built from two things. **Activities** describe what you offer, and **sessions** are when it happens. Set up an activity once, then put it on the calendar as often as you like. See [Core concepts](/getting-started/core-concepts/) for how they fit together.

## Create an activity

Go to **Activities** and choose **New activity**.

- **Name, description, colour and cover image** are what people see on your booking page.
- **Offer as** decides how it is booked:
  - **Class**: you put it on the calendar, and people book a seat.
  - **Appointment**: people pick a time from a coach's availability. See [Appointments & availability](/coaches/appointments/).
- **Who can book** and the prices come from your plans and your drop-in and trial settings. See [Offerings](/studio/offerings/).
- **Confirm bookings automatically** accepts bookings straight away. Switch it off to review each one.
- **What a visitor sees** adds the details people read before they book: prerequisites, meeting point, what's included, a cancellation policy and an FAQ.
- **Booking questions** ask something about each booking, such as "Any injuries today?".

You don't delete an activity you no longer run: you **archive** it, so its history stays intact.

## Put a class on the calendar

Open **Schedule** and choose **New → New class**. Pick the activity, then set:

- **Start** and **Duration**
- **Coach**, **Place** and **Room**
- **Max participants**, or leave it with no limit
- **Allow online booking**, so the class appears on your public booking page
- **Booking required**, if people must book in advance and can't just drop in

### Repeat it every week

Switch on **Repeat this session** to create a series:

- **Repeats**: daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, every N days, weeks or months
- **On**: the days of the week
- **Ends**: never, on a date, or after a number of sessions

A preview lists the upcoming sessions before you save.

## Change or delete one session

When you edit or delete a session that is part of a series, Linyup asks which ones you mean:

- **This session only**, for example a holiday or a one-off room change
- **This and all following sessions**, for example a new time from next month

Past sessions are never changed.

:::caution[People who booked are told]
Deleting a session emails a cancellation to everyone booked on it, with a link to book another time. People who paid for that session are always told.
:::

## Find your way around the schedule

- **Schedule** shows a calendar by **Day** or **Week**, and a **Month overview**.
- **List** shows **Upcoming** and **Past** sessions.
- Filter by type (classes, appointments, events), by activity, or by coach. **Only me** shows your own sessions.
- **Day sheet** gives a printable overview of the day.

## Events

An event is a one-off with its own registration, such as a camp, a seminar, a competition or a grading. It isn't an instance of an activity, so it has its own title, dates, location and optional fee. Create one from **Schedule → New → New event**.
