## What you offer

Everything a visitor can book or buy. Each item has its own base price, or none at all, before any benefit changes it.

- **Classes**: scheduled sessions people book a seat in
- **Appointments**: a coach's time, booked from their availability
- **Online courses**: self-paced lessons, sold or included
- **Shop items**: products and gift cards

## What you sell

The things people pay for once or on a schedule. Holding one of these is exactly what a member benefit checks for.

- **Memberships**: recurring plans, optionally capped per week
- **Class packs**: one-time credits, spent per booking
- **Gift cards**: a prepaid balance, spent at checkout (not on memberships or appointments)

## Member benefits

One rule on an offering says what holding a chosen plan changes about its price. There are three kinds:

- **Included**: holders book for free (a pack spends one credit instead)
- **Percentage off**: a reduced price for holders
- **Fixed member price**: a flat price that replaces the base one

With no benefit set, everyone pays the base price.

## The price preview

Pick anyone (a guest, a member, or a specific plan) and **Offer → Pricing** shows exactly what they would pay for every class, appointment, course and product. It uses the same logic as checkout.

- One persona at a time: guest, member, or a specific plan
- Every offering priced exactly as checkout would price it
- Health checks flag pricing that doesn't add up
