Common pricing setups. Each one covers how it works, where to set it up, what members see, and how to check it in **Offer → Pricing** before you switch it on.

## Sell a monthly membership

**How it works:** Create a subscription type under **Offer → Plans** with one or more prices (monthly, annual). Mark it public so it appears in your shop. Members buy it there and are billed automatically.

**What members see:** It appears on your shop's Memberships tab and in their member space.

**Check it:** Pick that plan's persona on Offer → Pricing.

## Cap a plan at N classes per week

**How it works:** On the subscription type, set a usage limit such as 3 per week. Covered bookings count against a weekly allowance that resets on Monday. Once it's used up, the member can still pay the drop-in price, at their member rate if you set one.

**What members see:** Coverage like "2 of 3 left". The 4th booking offers the drop-in.

**Check it:** The persona picker has an "allowance used up" switch.

## Let non-members pay per class

**How it works:** On a gated class, enable **Drop-in** with a price. Anyone not covered, guests included, can book that single class by paying.

**What members see:** Members covered by the class book free. Everyone else sees the drop-in price at booking.

**Check it:** Compare the Guest persona with a member persona.

## Give members a cheaper drop-in

**How it works:** In the class's drop-in section, add a member rate. Holders of the plans you choose pay a percentage off or a fixed price instead of the full drop-in. This is useful for plans that don't fully cover the class.

**What members see:** The original price struck through next to theirs.

**Check it:** Pick that plan's persona. The class row shows both prices.

## Sell a 10-class pack

**How it works:** Add a one-time price with credits (e.g. 10) to a subscription type, optionally with a validity window. Each covered booking spends one credit, and a cancelled booking refunds it.

**What members see:** Their remaining credits. Bookings say "1 credit".

**Check it:** The pack persona has a "pack used up" switch to preview the empty state.

## Offer newcomers a (paid) trial

**How it works:** On a gated class, enable the trial option. Leave the trial price empty for a free first class, or set an amount to charge for it. Each person gets one trial, ever, checked by email address.

**What members see:** Newcomers get a "First time here" option at booking.

**Check it:** The Guest persona shows "trial available" on gated classes.

## Price appointments

**How it works:** Appointments have no access gate: the price is the gate. Give each duration its own price (30/60/90 min), then optionally add a member benefit: included with certain plans, a percentage off, or a fixed member price. Unpriced durations are free for everyone.

**What members see:** Clients pick a time and see exactly their price. Covered members book free.

**Check it:** Each persona prices every duration on Offer → Pricing.

## Sell an online course

**How it works:** Set a course's access to **Sold** and give it a price. Buyers get lifetime access. Add a benefit so subscribers get it included or at a discount.

**What members see:** It appears in your shop's Courses tab, and owners watch it in their member space.

**Check it:** Course rows in the price preview show each persona's price.

## Sell gift cards

**How it works:** Enable gift cards and choose the amounts. Buyers receive a code by email. Anyone can redeem it, in part or in full, when paying for shop items, courses or drop-ins.

**What members see:** A Gift cards tab appears in your shop, and checkout gets a "Have a gift card?" field.

**Check it:** The Payments page lists sold cards and balances.

## Charge a fee after repeated no-shows

**How it works:** Enable the no-show policy with a fee and a threshold (e.g. CHF 15 after 3). Strikes count automatically when you mark bookings as no-shows. At the threshold, the member gets one fee with a payment link. You can always waive it.

**What members see:** An email with the fee. You track and waive fees under Payments.

**Check it:** Outstanding fees appear on the Payments page.

## Work with FitPass, Urban Sports Club & co.

**How it works:** Create a subscription type with source **Aggregator** and set what the partner pays you per visit. Assign it to partner members and include it in the classes they may attend. Each covered booking is recorded in a payout ledger instead of charging anyone.

**What members see:** Partner members book like regular members.

**Check it:** Payments shows partner visits and the month's estimated payout.
