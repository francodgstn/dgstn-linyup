Contacts are everyone your studio knows: leads, trialists, members and alumni, in one list. See [Core concepts](/getting-started/core-concepts/#contacts--everyone-your-studio-knows) for how they connect to everything else.

## The acquisition funnel

Contacts move through three stages. **The stage only ever moves forward.** It records the furthest point someone has reached, so it isn't a status you switch back and forth.

| Stage | What it means | Enters here from |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial booked** | They have reserved their first session but have not turned up yet. | Public booking page · paid drop-in · appointment booking · added by hand · "Place on funnel" |
| **Trial attended** | They showed up. Marking the first attendance promotes them automatically. | First attendance · walk-in at the desk · promoted by hand |
| **Joined** | They finished signup and are a member of the studio. | Completed signup · imported as an existing member · promoted by hand |

:::tip[Leads without a stage]
Someone can also arrive with **no stage at all**. A shop purchase or a form submission captures them as a lead outside the funnel. They join the funnel at _Trial booked_ with their first booking, or jump straight to _Trial attended_ the first time they show up.
:::

## Members who don't pay through Linyup

Not every member needs a subscription. Use an **affiliation** (member, trial, alumni) for people who belong but aren't billed in Linyup, such as kids billed on paper invoices or a founder who trains free. They show up in the right lists and can book members-only classes, with no billing attached.
