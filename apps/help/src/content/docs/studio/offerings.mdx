## Come at a time

Things people book for a date and a time.

- **Classes**: a seat in a session on your calendar. The class exists before anyone books it, and several people share it. See [Schedule & classes](/studio/schedule/).
- **Appointments**: a coach's time, one person at a time. Nothing is on the calendar until someone picks a slot from the coach's availability. See [Appointments](/coaches/appointments/).
- **Courses**: a set of lessons sold as one thing, such as "13 Wednesdays, 9 places, one price". Someone enrols once and has a place in every lesson, and a missed lesson doesn't free that place. A course can also be a weekend or a single afternoon.

:::note[Events]
Camps, workshops and gradings are [events](/studio/events/). They take registrations rather than payments, so they aren't on these shelves.
:::

## Take away

Things people buy whenever they like and keep.

- **Online courses**: lessons people watch on their own time, in their member space. Needs the Online courses plugin. Not the same thing as a course above.
- **Shop items**: products such as a T-shirt or a drinks bottle, sold in your [shop](/public/shop/). Needs the Products plugin.
- **Gift cards**: a prepaid amount that someone buys and anyone can spend at checkout. Needs the Gift cards plugin.

## Belong

Plans people hold. Holding one is what opens the other two shelves, for free or at a member price.

- **Memberships**: plans that renew, weekly to yearly. They can include classes, optionally capped at a number per week.
- **Class packs**: a set of credits paid for once, such as 10 classes. Each booking spends one credit, and a cancelled booking gives it back.

Memberships and class packs are paid for too, of course: once, or on a schedule. They're the plans the first two columns refer to.

## What changes a price

Each thing on the shelves has a base price, or none. Three things can change what a particular person pays.

- **Member benefits.** On any class, appointment, course or online course, choose which plans get it **included** (no cost) or at a **member price** (a percentage off, or a fixed price). With no benefit set, everyone pays the base price.
- **Trials.** A class can let newcomers try it once, free or at a trial price, even when it's otherwise only for members.
- **Promo codes.** A code lowers the price at checkout, for the things and the people you choose. See [Promo codes & gift cards](/studio/promo-codes/).

A gift card isn't a discount: it's money someone already paid, spent at checkout like cash.

## Check it before you switch it on

**Manage → Pricing** shows what anyone would pay. Pick a guest, a member, or a specific plan, and every class, appointment, online course and shop item is priced exactly as checkout would price it.

- One person at a time: a guest, a member, or a specific plan
- Uses the same rules as checkout, so there are no surprises
- Health checks flag pricing that doesn't add up, such as a class nobody can book
