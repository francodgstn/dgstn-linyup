Memberships and class packs are paid for too, of course: once, or on a schedule. They're the plans the first two columns refer to.

## What changes a price

Each thing on the shelves has a base price, or none. Three things can change what a particular person pays.

- **Member benefits.** On any class, appointment, course or online course, choose which plans get it **included** (no cost) or at a **member price** (a percentage off, or a fixed price). With no benefit set, everyone pays the base price.
- **Trials.** A class can let newcomers try it once, free or at a trial price, even when it's otherwise only for members.
- **Promo codes.** A code lowers the price at checkout, for the things and the people you choose. See [Promo codes & gift cards](/studio/promo-codes/).

A gift card isn't a discount: it's money someone already paid, spent at checkout like cash.

## Check it before you switch it on

**Manage → Pricing** shows what anyone would pay. Pick a guest, a member, or a specific plan, and every class, appointment, online course and shop item is priced exactly as checkout would price it.

- One person at a time: a guest, a member, or a specific plan
- Uses the same rules as checkout, so there are no surprises
- Health checks flag pricing that doesn't add up, such as a class nobody can book
