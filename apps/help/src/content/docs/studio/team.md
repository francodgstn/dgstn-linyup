Everyone who helps run your studio gets their own login, with a **role** that decides what they can see and change.

:::note[Plans]
The Free and Coach plans are for one person. To invite others, you need the Studio plan or above.
:::

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything, including your Linyup plan and billing, plugins, integrations and deleting the studio. Only owners change roles or remove people. |
| **Manager** | Runs the studio day to day: contacts, schedule, prices, payments. Everything except studio settings, billing, integrations and plugins. |
| **Coach** | Looks after their own people and their own schedule. By default a coach can see and edit contacts, see the full calendar, and create and edit sessions. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to the studio. |

The **coach** role is the one you can adjust. Under **Settings → Roles & permissions** you can give coaches more access or less. A coach can never manage billing, plugins, integrations or the team.

### "Also a coach"

Anyone can teach, whatever their role. Switch on **Also a coach** for a person and they appear in the coach list and the session's coach picker, and contacts can be assigned to them. An owner who teaches is an owner with **Also a coach** switched on.

## Invite someone

- **Team**: choose **Invite member**, enter their email and pick a role. Only owners can invite managers and viewers.
- **Coaches**: choose **Invite coach** to invite someone straight into the coach role.

They get an email, **Invitation to join {your studio} on Linyup**. They sign in or create an account, then choose **Accept invitation**. The invitation is valid for **7 days**. Until then it shows as _Pending_ on the Team screen, where you can cancel it or send it again.

## Coaches and appointments

For appointments, each coach publishes their own availability, and clients book time with a specific coach. See [Appointments & availability](/coaches/appointments/).
