Appointments work the other way round from classes. **Nothing sits on the calendar until someone books.** You publish your _availability_ (the windows you are free), and a session is created the moment a client takes a slot. Each slot takes one booking.

## One offering, not one per class

It's tempting to mirror every class you teach as a separate private offering. Think about what is actually being sold instead: an hour of your attention. The price and the length are the same either way, and what happens in the hour is agreed on the day.

So a studio usually creates **one** appointment activity, for example _Personal training_, and each coach publishes availability against it. Add a second appointment activity only when it is a genuinely different product, such as a free 20-minute intro call next to a paid 60-minute session. Those differ in price and in who may book.

:::tip[The test]
Different terms, different offering. Same terms, one offering.
:::

## Pricing

Each duration can have its own price, and member benefits can make it included or cheaper for certain plans. See [Price appointments](/studio/pricing-recipes/#price-appointments).
