The dashboard is where your day starts. It answers two questions: _what's on today_, and _who needs me_.

## Today

The **Today** panel lists the day's sessions, with how many people have booked, how many are trials and how many have attended. It tells you when a class is **Starting now** or **In N min**. Open a session to see its roster and run [check-in](/coaches/check-in/).

Use **Previous day** and **Next day** to look ahead, and **Back to today** to return.

## Waiting on you

**Waiting on you** gathers the people who need a reply or a nudge, each with the reason:

- a new lead nobody has replied to yet
- a trial booked but not attended yet
- someone who used to come and has stopped
- a subscription that is set to end
- an open alert on someone's record, or a sign-up request to process

When there is nothing left, it says **All clear**.

## Quick actions

**New session** and **New contact** are always at the top of the dashboard. On the schedule, the **Only me** filter shows just your own sessions.

## As a coach

If your role is **coach**, the dashboard shows the contacts assigned to you rather than the whole studio. What you can change is set by the studio's owner. See [Team & roles](/studio/team/).
