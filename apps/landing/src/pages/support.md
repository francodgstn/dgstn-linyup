# Support

Most questions about the Linyup app have one of two answers, and knowing which
saves you a round trip.

## Ask your studio

Your studio, coach or club owns everything about your membership. They are the
right people for:

- Bookings, cancellations and waiting lists
- Class times, locations and who is teaching
- Prices, subscriptions, credits and invoices
- Your rank, attendance record or anything else on your profile

Linyup makes the software; your studio runs it. We cannot change a booking or a
price on your behalf, and we will not — those are theirs to decide.

## Ask us

Email **hello@linyup.com** for anything about the app itself:

- You cannot sign in
- The app crashes, freezes or will not load
- Something is displayed wrong, or a button does nothing
- You want your account deleted — see [Delete your account](/delete-account)

To save time, include the email address you use with your studio, the name of
your studio, and your phone model. If something looks wrong on screen, a
screenshot is worth several paragraphs.

We answer every message.

## Signing in

There is no password. You enter your email address and we send you a six-digit
code.

If the code does not arrive, check your spam folder first. If it is not there,
the usual cause is that your studio has a different address on file for you than
the one you typed — ask them which one they have, and use that.

You need an invitation from a studio that uses Linyup before you can sign in at
all. If no studio has invited you, there is nothing in the app for you yet.

## Coaches and studio owners

If you run a studio on Linyup rather than train at one, manage your business at
[linyup.com](/) and email the same address — say that you are an account owner
and we will pick it up from there.
